Hi, I'm Anna —
maker of Little Big Minds

I trained at art school, and ever since, a love of beautiful things has quietly kept growing in me. Then I became a mother — and discovered how much of the world I could open up for my son, one small moment at a time.

I also know the honest truth: not every parent has the time, energy or resources to sit down and do this every day. So I gathered it all up — the activities my son genuinely loves, and the ones I saw with my own eyes really help him grow — and turned them into bundles you can simply print.

That's Little Big Minds: made by a mum, shaped by an artist, tested by one very busy little boy. 🤍

— Anna
