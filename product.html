Scissors & glue are only needed for a few cut-and-glue pages.

Format & printing

A4 PDF — 31 pages (cover + 30 activities). Downloaded instantly after checkout.

  • Print in colour for best results
  • Print single-sided
  • Set scale to 100% / "fit to page"
  • Opens on any device with a free PDF reader

Age & independence

Made for ages 3–5. Most activities can be done fairly independently by 4–5 year-olds; the cutting & gluing pages are best with a grown-up, and 3-year-olds will enjoy the whole set with a little help.

A guide based on the activity types — every child is different.

How long it takes

Roughly 5–10 minutes per activity (a rough guide, not a rule) — best enjoyed over many small sittings rather than all at once.
